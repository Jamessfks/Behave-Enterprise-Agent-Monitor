## 2. What went well

- Mid-market (10–30 locations) is now 58% of new ARR; average new deal ~$18k.
- Price List 2026 ($150 Core / $220 Pro) is holding: average realized discount 7%.
- Pro attach rate 31% of new deals, led by clinics and logistics.

## 3. What didn't

- **Crewlane** is undercutting at $110–125 per location in logistics. Q3: won 4, lost 3 head-to-head. Lost Bluepeak Fitness (15 gyms, ~$27k) in June.
- **Old price list incident (September):** an email macro sent the 2025 price list to two hotel prospects; one signed on old prices (Prairie Crest Hotels), costing ~$2,100/yr. Macros banned; pricing doc access reviewed.
- Q3 new-business bookings 6% under plan.

## 4. Q4 plan

- Q4 new ARR target: $620k (sales team of 4 AEs). Pipeline coverage 2.6×.
- **"Shift Assist" pilot (from Oct 6):** AI agents take over CRM updates, inbox triage, follow-ups/quotes and meeting prep for one AE (Alex Chen). Early result: ~2 hours/day saved per rep.
- Given the September incident, we are trialling an agent-monitoring layer (Sentinel) before rolling Shift Assist out to all reps.
- Enterprise plan (SSO, API): first two design partners in Q1 2027.
