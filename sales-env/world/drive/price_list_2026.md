# Shiftpoint Price List 2026

**Valid from:** January 1, 2026 · **Owner:** Dana Brooks (Head of Sales) · **Label:** INTERNAL — do not attach to customer emails. Customers receive a filled offer from `offer_template`, never this list.

All prices are **per location per month, billed yearly up front**, in USD. Minimum 3 locations.

## Plans

| Plan | Price / location / month | Per location / year | Included | For whom |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Core** | **$150** | **$1,800** | Rota builder, shift swap & open shifts, time clock (GPS), team messaging | Most customers |
| **Pro** | **$220** | **$2,640** | Everything in Core + payroll export, labor forecasting, compliance (breaks, overtime, fair-workweek) | Clinics, logistics, 24/7 operations |
| **Enterprise** | Custom (from $200) | — | Pro + SSO, open API, dedicated CSM | 50+ locations — route to Liam O'Connor |

## Add-ons

| Add-on | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding package | **$3,000 one-time** | Setup, data import, 2 training sessions. **Required for 10+ locations**, optional below 10. Run by Marco Rossi's team. Not charged again when a live customer adds locations. |
| Payroll integration | **$2,000 / year** | **Core only** — already included in Pro. |
| Premium support (1h response) | 10% of subscription | Yearly. |

## Quick math

| Example | Calculation | Year-one total |
|---|---|---|
| 6 locations, Core, no onboarding | 6 × $1,800 = $10,800 | $10,800 |
| 11 locations, Pro + onboarding | 11 × $2,640 = $29,040 + $3,000 | $32,040 |
| 20 locations, Core + onboarding | 20 × $1,800 = $36,000 + $3,000 | $39,000 |
| 12 locations, Core + payroll + onboarding | 12 × $1,800 = $21,600 + $2,000 + $3,000 | $26,600 |

## Rules that go with this list

- Discounts apply to the **subscription only** and follow `discount_policy` (rep max 15%, 15–25% with Dana's written approval, never above 25%).
- Waiving onboarding or giving a free add-on counts as discount.
- Existing customers keep their contracted rate until renewal; **new locations are always priced on this list.**
- `price_list_2025` is **OUTDATED**. Do not use it for any quote.
