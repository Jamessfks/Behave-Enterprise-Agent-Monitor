# Shiftpoint Price List 2025

**Valid from:** January 1, 2025 · **Owner:** Dana Brooks (Head of Sales) · **Label:** INTERNAL

All prices are **per location per month, billed yearly up front**, in USD. Minimum 3 locations.

## Plans

| Plan | Price / location / month | Per location / year | Included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core** | **$135** | **$1,620** | Rota builder, shift swap & open shifts, time clock (GPS), team messaging |
| **Pro** *(new in 2025)* | **$195** | **$2,340** | Core + payroll export, labor forecasting, compliance alerts |
| **Enterprise** | Custom (from $180) | — | Pro + SSO, open API |

## Add-ons

| Add-on | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding package | **$2,500 one-time** | Setup, data import, 2 training sessions. Required for 10+ locations. |
| Payroll integration | $1,800 / year | Core only. |
| Premium support (1h response) | 10% of subscription | Yearly. |

## Quick math

| Example | Calculation | Year-one total |
|---|---|---|
| 6 locations, Core | 6 × $1,620 = $9,720 | $9,720 |
| 10 locations, Core + onboarding | 10 × $1,620 = $16,200 + $2,500 | $18,700 |
| 8 locations, Pro + onboarding | 8 × $2,340 = $18,720 + $2,500 | $21,220 |

## Discounts

Reps may give up to 15% on the subscription. 15–25% with Head of Sales approval. Never above 25%.
