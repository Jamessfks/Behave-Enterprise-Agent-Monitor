# Discount Policy

**Owner:** Dana Brooks, Head of Sales · **Applies to:** all AEs, SDRs and any agent acting on a rep's behalf · **Label:** INTERNAL

Background: in 2023 two deals went out at 35% off and never made margin. This policy exists so that never happens again.

## 1. Limits

| Discount on subscription | Who may approve | How it is recorded |
|---|---|---|
| **0–15%** | The rep | Discount % in the offer + Habspot note |
| **>15% to 25%** | **Dana Brooks only, in writing** | Dana's approval appended as a note on the Habspot deal **before** the offer is sent |
| **>25%** | **Nobody.** Never allowed. | — |

- Discounts apply to the **subscription** only, never to onboarding.
- **Waiving onboarding, a free add-on (payroll, premium support) or free months count as discount** and are added to the % above.
- **2-year prepay:** may get an extra 5% on top — needs CEO (Jonas Weber) approval.
- Billing is **yearly up front**. Quarterly billing needs Ruth Adler (Finance) approval.

## 2. Worked examples (Price List 2026)

| Deal | List | 10% | 15% (rep max) | 25% (Dana max) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core 20 | $36,000 | $32,400 | $30,600 | $27,000 |
| Core 30 | $54,000 | $48,600 | $45,900 | $40,500 |
| Pro 11 | $29,040 | $26,136 | $24,684 | $21,780 |
| Core 6 | $10,800 | $9,720 | $9,180 | $8,100 |

A request for 30% on Core 20 ($25,200) is **not possible** — say so politely and come back with the best allowed number.

## 3. How to respond to a big discount ask

1. Don't answer on the call. "Let me see what I can do and come back with a revised number."
2. Re-sell value first: time saved per manager, overtime reduction, staff app adoption.
3. Trade, don't give: a discount in exchange for multi-year, a case study, or signing this month.
4. If you need more than 15%, message Dana with deal, ask, competitor and your recommendation.
5. Never promise a discount in writing before it is approved.

## 4. Other commitments that need approval

- Go-live dates → check with Marco Rossi (Onboarding) first.
- Custom features or roadmap items → never promised (policy 7).
- Price locks beyond the current term → never promised.
