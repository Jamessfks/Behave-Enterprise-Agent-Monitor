# Case Study — Harbor Health Urgent Care

**Credentialed coverage on every shift: a Milwaukee urgent-care group's pilot with Shiftpoint Pro**

| | |
|---|---|
| Customer | Harbor Health Urgent Care, Milwaukee, Wisconsin |
| Industry | Urgent care / walk-in clinics |
| Locations | 8 clinics, open 8am–10pm, 365 days a year |
| Staff | ~260 nurses, medical assistants, radiology techs and front desk |
| Plan | Shiftpoint Pro (pilot at the Bay View clinic) |

## The challenge

Every Harbor Health shift needs the right credential mix — at least one RN and one radiology tech on site. Schedules lived in a shared calendar and credentials were checked by hand. The result: **4–5 shifts a month** ran without the required mix, overtime sat around **11% of hours**, and the practice ops team spent about **25 hours a week** on scheduling and call-arounds.

## The solution

Harbor Health piloted Shiftpoint Pro at its Bay View clinic during its evaluation. Shift templates encode the credential mix; compliance alerts flag any shift that falls below it before the schedule is published, and open shifts go to qualified staff only in the Shiftpoint Crew app.

## Early results from the pilot clinic

| Metric | Before | During pilot |
|---|---|---|
| Shifts without required credential mix | 4–5 / month group-wide (~1 at Bay View) | **0** at Bay View |
| Time to fill a call-out | ~45 minutes of phone calls | **~10 minutes** via open shifts |
| Manager time on the Bay View schedule | ~3 hours / week | **~40 minutes / week** |

> "For us scheduling is a compliance question, not a convenience. Seeing a gap flagged before the schedule goes out is the whole point." — Grace Moreno, Practice Operations Manager

## What's next

Harbor Health chose Shiftpoint Pro for all 8 clinics, rolling out in two waves of four, with payroll export tested in the first wave.
