## 2. Company story

Bluepeak opened its first gym in Westport in 2014 and grew to 15 gyms across both Kansas City metros. Staff are mostly students and part-timers who swap shifts constantly, around **300 swaps a month** chain-wide. In spring 2026 Bluepeak evaluated Shiftpoint (Core 15, $27,000) against Crewlane and picked Crewlane on price (~$115/location/month) on 2026-06-20.

Four months in, the Crewlane rollout is struggling. Chris Yoon, a newly hired Regional Manager for the eight Kansas-side gyms, says **only ~55% of staff use the Crewlane app**, the **no-show rate on 5am opening shifts is ~12%**, and his gym managers still spend **~4 hours a week** each on scheduling. He is re-opening the conversation on his own initiative. Lena Ortiz, the General Manager who ran the original deal, asked on 2026-07-02 to be removed from all lists.

**Gyms:** Westport, Crossroads, Plaza, Brookside, Waldo, North Kansas City, Liberty, Lee's Summit, Independence (Missouri side); Overland Park, Olathe, Lenexa, Shawnee, Mission, Leawood (Kansas side, Chris's region).
