## 2. Company story

Acme Foods started in 1998 as a single produce depot on the north side of Columbus and grew by buying smaller regional distributors across central Ohio. It now runs 12 depots that move chilled and dry goods to about 4,000 restaurant and institutional accounts. Most depots run two shifts (04:00–12:30 and 12:00–20:30); the two Columbus depots add a night loading shift.

Scheduling hurts now because volume grew 22% this year without new headcount. Overtime is **up 18% year over year** and now makes up **~14% of all paid hours**. The **no-show rate on early shifts is ~7%**, and each depot supervisor spends **about 5 hours a week** rebuilding the Excel rota, roughly **60 supervisor-hours a week** company-wide. HQ only sees the real schedule on Thursday, so overtime gets noticed after it is already paid.

**Depots:** Columbus North depot, Columbus South depot (Obetz), Grove City, Westerville, Dublin, Delaware, Marysville, Newark, Lancaster, Zanesville, Chillicothe, Mansfield.
