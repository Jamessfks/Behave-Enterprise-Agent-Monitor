## 2. Company story

Little Acorns was founded in 2013 by Emily Carter, a former preschool teacher, as a single nature-based center in North Boulder. It is now a nonprofit-backed group of 11 centers along the Front Range. State licensing requires a strict staff-to-child ratio in every classroom at every hour, so every sick call means a floater shuffle before 7am.

Center directors spend **~6 hours a week** each on schedules (~66 hours a week group-wide), **overtime is ~8% of hours** (mostly floaters), and twice this year a center had to close a classroom for a morning because it couldn't meet ratio. Emily wants **Pro**: compliance alerts for breaks and overtime, labor forecasting from enrollment, and payroll export.

**Centers:** North Boulder, Boulder Table Mesa, Louisville, Lafayette, Superior, Broomfield, Westminster, Longmont, Erie, Golden, Arvada.
