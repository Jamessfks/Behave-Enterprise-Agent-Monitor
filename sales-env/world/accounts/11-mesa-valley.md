## 2. Company story

Mesa Valley was founded in 2017 by a family-medicine group that opened a walk-in clinic in the Northeast Heights. It now runs five clinics across Albuquerque and Rio Rancho, open 8am–8pm daily. A sixth clinic is planned for late 2027 but not confirmed.

Nora Kim builds every rota herself. She says she spends **"most of Sunday", about 8 hours a week**, on schedules, **overtime is ~10% of hours**, and **3–4 shifts a month** start without an X-ray tech because a swap fell through in the group text. She found Shiftpoint through a Google search and the Harbor Health case study.

**Clinics:** Northeast Heights, Nob Hill, Westside (Cottonwood), Downtown, Rio Rancho.
