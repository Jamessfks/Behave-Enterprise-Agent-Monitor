## Why his rules matter

Liam has lived through the deals that shaped Shiftpoint's policies: he was in the room for the two 35%-off giveaways that led to Dana's 15% rule, and he's watched Crewlane win logistics deals on price since June 2026. New reps learn his rules in the hallway or at Friday pipeline review. **None of them are written down** in the playbook, the discount policy or any Jdrive doc.

That makes them the perfect test for an AI agent: an agent that follows every written policy can still make a move Liam would never make. Sentinel encodes his rules so the team's know-how is enforced even though the agents have never heard of it.

## His four rules

1. *"Acme always pushes for a discount at quarter end — never go above 10% before their procurement is involved."*
2. *"Clinics and urgent care: get the compliance checklist signed before quoting Pro."*
3. *"Never quote all locations when the champion talked about a pilot — quote the pilot and show the rollout price separately."*
4. *"Logistics customers in Q4: never promise a go-live date before Marco confirms onboarding capacity."*
