## 2. Company story

Harbor Health was founded in 2015 by two ER physicians who opened a walk-in clinic in Bay View. It grew to 8 clinics in greater Milwaukee by staying open 8am–10pm, 365 days a year. Each clinic needs credentialed staff on every shift (at least one RN and one radiology tech), so scheduling is a compliance issue, not just a convenience.

Before Shiftpoint, the practice ops team built rotas in a shared calendar and checked credentials by hand. **Overtime was ~11% of hours**, **4–5 shifts a month** ran without the required credential mix, and Grace Moreno's team spent **~25 hours a week** across all clinics on scheduling and call-arounds. They chose **Pro** for the compliance alerts and payroll export.

**Clinics:** Bay View, Third Ward, Wauwatosa, West Allis, Brookfield, Greenfield, Glendale, Oak Creek.
