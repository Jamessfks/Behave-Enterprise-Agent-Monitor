## 2. Company story

SparklePro started in 2008 as a husband-and-wife office cleaning business in Omaha and expanded by opening branches in Nebraska, Iowa and Kansas. Each branch dispatches crews to dozens of client buildings every night. Staff turnover is high (~70% a year), and unfilled shifts mean a client building goes uncleaned and a credit note follows.

Kevin O'Brien became COO in 2025 and found that **~6% of shifts go uncovered** at short notice, **client credits for missed cleans cost ~$90k last year**, and branch leads spend **~6 hours a week** each on rotas and call-arounds (~85 hours a week total). He wants GPS clock-in at client sites and open shifts that crews can pick up in the app.

**Branches:** Omaha Central, Omaha West, Papillion, Bellevue, Lincoln North, Lincoln South, Grand Island, Kearney, Council Bluffs, Des Moines, Ames, Sioux City, Kansas City KS, Topeka.
