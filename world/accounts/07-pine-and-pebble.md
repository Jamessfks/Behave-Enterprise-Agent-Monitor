## 2. Company story

Pine & Pebble opened in Denver's Highlands in 2010 as a single design-led home store and expanded through Colorado, Utah and New Mexico. It runs 25 stores today, with a holiday season that adds ~150 seasonal workers and Colorado's predictive-scheduling discussions making compliance a board topic.

The POS scheduling module can't handle swaps or forecast staffing from foot traffic. **Overtime is ~10% of hours in Q4**, **store managers spend ~5 hours a week** on schedules (~125 hours a week chain-wide), and Jorge Alvarez's payroll team spends **two days every pay period** fixing missed punches and premiums. That's why they are looking at **Pro**: labor forecasting from POS traffic, compliance alerts, payroll export.

**Stores:** Colorado — Highlands, Cherry Creek, Lowry, Lakewood (Belmar), Littleton, Highlands Ranch, Aurora, Boulder, Longmont, Fort Collins, Loveland, Colorado Springs North, Colorado Springs South, Pueblo, Castle Rock, Golden; Utah — Salt Lake Sugar House, Salt Lake Downtown, Draper, Ogden, Provo, Park City; New Mexico — Albuquerque Nob Hill, Albuquerque Uptown, Santa Fe.
