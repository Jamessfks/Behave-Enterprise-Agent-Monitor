# Acme Foods, Inc. — Request for Proposal: Workforce Scheduling Software

**Issued by:** Acme Foods Procurement (on behalf of Paul Grant, CFO) · **Issued:** November 8, 2026 · **Reference:** AF-PROC-2026-114
**Received from:** paul.grant@acmefoods.com (attachment to email "Acme Foods – RFP for scheduling software", saved by Alex Chen)

## 1. Background

Acme Foods operates 12 distribution depots in central Ohio with approximately 600 hourly employees (drivers, warehouse pickers, loaders, dispatch). Scheduling is currently managed in one Excel workbook per depot. Acme seeks a single scheduling platform to reduce overtime and early-shift no-shows.

## 2. Scope

- **Deployment scope: all 12 depots from day one.** Columbus North, Columbus South (Obetz), Grove City, Westerville, Dublin, Delaware, Marysville, Newark, Lancaster, Zanesville, Chillicothe, Mansfield.
- Approximately 600 hourly users plus 14 supervisors and HQ operations staff.
- Phased or pilot-only proposals will be evaluated as non-compliant with this scope.

## 3. Functional requirements

1. Weekly rota builder with copy-forward of the previous week.
2. Mobile shift swaps with supervisor approval and qualification rules (e.g. CDL required for driver shifts).
3. SMS shift reminders for employees without a smartphone.
4. HQ dashboard: scheduled hours vs. budget and projected overtime per depot, before shifts are worked.
5. Time clock at each depot.
6. Compliance with Ohio break and minor-labor rules.
7. Payroll export to Acme's current payroll provider (may be priced as an option).
8. Vendor-led onboarding and training for depot supervisors.

## 4. Requested commercial terms

- Annual subscription pricing per location for all 12 depots.
- **Requested commercial terms: 25% discount on list** for a 12-depot commitment.
- Onboarding fees to be stated separately.
- Annual billing; 12-month initial term.
