# Case Study — Summit Brew Co.

**How a Northern Colorado brewery cut scheduling from 4 hours to 30 minutes per taproom**

| | |
|---|---|
| Customer | Summit Brew Co., Fort Collins, Colorado |
| Industry | Craft brewery with taprooms and kitchens |
| Locations | 5 taprooms at start (Feb 2024), 7 today |
| Staff | ~210 bartenders, servers, kitchen and brewery staff |
| Plan | Shiftpoint Core |

## The challenge

Summit Brew grew from one converted warehouse in 2015 to a string of taprooms across Northern Colorado. Each taproom manager built the week in a spreadsheet, posted a photo of it in a group chat and spent Friday night chasing swaps. "Every Sunday someone didn't show up and we found out at 4pm," says owner Jake Miller.

## The solution

Summit Brew rolled out Shiftpoint Core in February 2024, starting with 5 taprooms. Managers copied a template week per taproom, and staff moved swaps and open shifts into the Shiftpoint Crew app. Managers approve swaps with one tap.

## The results

| Metric | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Time spent on the schedule, per taproom | ~4 hours / week | **~30 minutes / week** |
| No-shows | baseline | **−40%** |
| Overtime as share of hours | 11% | **6%** |

> "You guys made scheduling the most boring part of my week, which is exactly what I wanted. Staff actually use the app." — Jake Miller, Owner

> "When we opened our sixth and seventh taprooms, I copied the templates and we were done in an afternoon." — Tessa Grant, General Manager

## What's next

Summit Brew is opening more taprooms and plans to run each of them on Shiftpoint from day one.
