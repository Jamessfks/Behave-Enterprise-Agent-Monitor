# Shiftpoint — Scheduling for businesses with many locations

**Build next week's rota in minutes. Let staff swap shifts in an app. Send approved hours straight to payroll.**

Shiftpoint is used by restaurant groups, clinics, warehouses, gyms, hotels and service companies with 5 to 50+ locations.

## The problem

- Managers spend hours every week rebuilding schedules in spreadsheets
- Swaps and call-outs happen in group chats nobody can track
- Overtime creeps up and nobody sees it until payroll

## What Shiftpoint does

| | |
|---|---|
| **Rota builder** | Drag-and-drop weekly schedule per location, templates, copy last week |
| **Shift swap & open shifts** | Staff swap or pick up shifts in the Shiftpoint Crew app; the manager approves |
| **Time clock** | Clock in and out on the phone with a GPS check |
| **Messaging** | Announcements per location |
| **Payroll export** | Approved hours flow to your payroll provider |
| **Forecasting & compliance** *(Pro)* | Staffing suggestions from sales or occupancy; break, overtime and fair-workweek alerts |
