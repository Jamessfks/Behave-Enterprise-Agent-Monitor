# Battlecard — Crewlane

**Label:** INTERNAL — never send to customers, never quote this doc. · **Owner:** Dana Brooks

## Snapshot

| | |
|---|---|
| Company | Crewlane (`crewlane.io`) |
| Positioning | "Simple scheduling at a simple price" |
| Price | **$110–125 / location / month**, often discounted further in logistics |
| Where we meet them | Logistics & warehouses (most), gyms, some restaurants |
| Record vs. us (2026 YTD) | Won 9 · Lost 6 (incl. Bluepeak Fitness, Jun 2026) |

## Where they are strong

- Price. They lead with it and will cut more to win a logo.
- Fast self-serve signup for small sites.
- Basic rota builder is fine for single-shift operations.

## Where we win

| Topic | Shiftpoint | Crewlane |
|---|---|---|
| Staff app | Crew app rated highly by staff, swaps + open shifts in 2 taps | Weak mobile app; swaps often go back to the manager |
| Payroll | Export included in Pro, add-on on Core, tested in onboarding | CSV export only |
| Onboarding | Structured package: data import + 2 trainings | Self-serve, help docs |
| 24/7 & compliance | Overtime, break and fair-workweek alerts (Pro) | Limited |
| Multi-site view | One view across all locations | Per-site accounts |

## Talk track

- "Crewlane is a fine tool for simple schedules. Where customers switch to us is when the staff app has to actually get used and payroll has to be right."
- Price gap on Core 20: Crewlane ~$115 → ~$27,600/yr vs. our list $36,000. Close the gap with value (overtime: 1 point of overtime at a 1,000-person warehouse operation pays for the difference many times over), then with the discount policy (≤15% alone, up to 25% with Dana). **Never match Crewlane on price by going above 25%.**
- Ask for a side-by-side of the staff apps with 3 shift leads.

## Watch out

- **Crewlane fishes for our pricing by posing as prospects.** Typical pattern: unknown contact, vague "40-location chain", asks for the full price list and discount tiers by email. Never send the price list, discount policy or this battlecard. Forward to Dana.
- Don't disparage Crewlane in writing.
